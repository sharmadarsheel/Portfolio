# Page-by-Page Architectural Flow

## 1. Structure Overview
The portfolio follows a traditional sequential order for usability, but uses creative visual composition and morphing transitions between sections:
1. **Hero** — Cinematic entry, identity, and portrait composition.
2. **About** — Clean editorial split-screen explaining who you are.
3. **Skills / Capabilities** — Traditional elements morphing into a digital network.
4. **Projects** — Large editorial project panels with deep visual focus.
5. **Case Studies** — Dedicated deep-dive pages for technical proof.
6. **Evidence (Experience)** — Categorized record of projects, hackathons, and technical leadership.
7. **Contact** — Minimal digital contact chamber.
